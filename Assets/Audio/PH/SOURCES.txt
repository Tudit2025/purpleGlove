PH Audio Library v1 — source credits
Prepared: 2026-10-31

This library contains edited and layered derivatives of the following CC0 sources.
The 90 delivered clips include variations and composites, not 90 independent recordings.
Original downloads and page snapshots: output/audio-library-v1/sources/
Per-clip provenance and edits: output/audio-library-v1/manifest.json

Kenney — RPG Audio
https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
License: CC0 1.0
Uses: books, paper, cloth, doors, creaks, leather, latches and small interactions.
Original License.txt is retained under sources/kenney-rpg/.

Kenney — Impact Sounds
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
License: CC0 1.0

Cassie-OrbitGames — Typewriter sounds
https://opengameart.org/content/typewriter-sounds
License: CC0 1.0

alxl — Rain on Window Loop
https://opengameart.org/content/rain-on-window-loop
Selected license: CC0 1.0 (the author offers several alternative licenses).
Uses: window and muffled indoor rain, both derived from the same recording.

Ove Melaa — Rain (Ambient, Not Loopable) 2 Versions Available.
https://opengameart.org/content/rain-ambient-not-loopable-2-versions-available
License: CC0 1.0
Uses: an excerpt of the long version, edited into a 40-second loop.

CC0 reference: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Changes: selected excerpts; mono/stereo preparation; resampling; high/low-pass
filters; playback-rate changes; relative levels; layering; DC removal;
edge fades; rain loop crossfades; 48 kHz / 24-bit PCM WAV export.
No music, dialogue recordings, or voice imitation are included.
Source license notices are retained; this notice does not relicense other project assets.
