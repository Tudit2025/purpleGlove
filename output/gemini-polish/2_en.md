# The Purple Glove · Text Localization · Part 2: Documents & Books

## Notes for Localizers

This file contains the in-game documents, forms, prayers, and notebook pages for *The Purple Glove*. 
- All blockquotes (`>`) preserve the exact line count of the Chinese original so that page layouts and UI containers remain intact.
- Dynamic strings (`Retain in Guild Seal`, `Deliver to Milan`, `Deliver to Venice`, `Deliver to France`, and `○` / `●`) match the keys in Part 3.
- Cipher examples, equations, and English/French source texts are kept unaltered.

### 04 行会会长的信

出现时机：第一幕首批来件。

> Luca:
>
> Milan seeks to seize the border granaries on the strength of
> this dispatch; Venice refuses to yield its supply lines and
> garrisons to a neighbor, yet dares not ignore a war report;
> France refuses to let others declare war in its name. All
> three await your verification, each hoping it speaks for them.
>
> The Guild prints their decrees and holds their deeds; now even
> our verification is a prize in their struggle. Delay, and they
> accuse us of obstructing military affairs; sign blindly, and
> they will blame the Guild when the truth comes out. We rely on
> all three for our livelihood, yet our craftsmen must not become
> their scapegoats.
>
> Because they distrust one another, they entrusted this to a
> jointly registered Verifier. For this dispatch, you alone hold
> three-party recognition, investigative authority, and a formal
> voice tonight. You may requisition records, question the envoys,
> and enter your findings into the council roll; not even I may
> sign in your stead. This right cannot settle their dispute, but
> it keeps unresolved doubts on paper, safe from hasty pressure.
>
> Should trouble arise tonight, I authorize you to decide whether
> to entrust this case's archived documents to Milan, Venice, or
> France. On the enclosed transfer application, list the archive
> number, documents, and chosen recipient; sign it and give it to
> the Clerk to arrange the handover. If no party can be trusted,
> you may keep the files sealed. This is an emergency measure.
> Watch closely who seeks the originals, choose with care, and
> obtain a receipt for any handover.
>
> I know you worry for Matteo. May what you uncover tonight earn
> him the chance to speak for himself. Where doubts remain, do
> not sign a guarantee for anyone.
>
> Master of the Printing Guild

> IV · Vigenère
>

#### 第 11 页

> V · Affine Cipher
>
> A=0, B=1 … Z=25
> y = (ax+b) mod 26
>
> 'a' must be coprime to 26
> for unique decryption.
>
> Personal practice mark:

>
> Ex: a=5, b=8

#### 第 12 页

>
> Plaintext A is 0.
> 5×0+8 = 8, yielding I.
>
> Inverse: 21×(8-8)=0,
> restoring A.
>
> If result is negative,
> add 26 until positive.
>
> Retains monoalphabetic
> frequency; not foolproof.

>
> Decryption adds or drops
> no letters; it restores order.
